FAQ

Straight answers before you reserve.

How do I know a competitor won't be on my card? +

Once a business reserves a spot in an industry category, that category is closed for that card. No other business in the same line of work is added to it.

What locks in my spot? +

A paid invoice. Once that's in, your category on that card is yours.

What if I want to be on both routes? +

You can reserve a spot on El Paseo, Sun City Palm Desert, or both. Each route is its own card and its own reservation.

Do I need to design anything myself? +

No. We build your spot on the card and send it to you to approve before it goes to print.

How many homes actually get the mailer? +

Around 5,000 homes per route, mailed by USPS — see the full mailing area report for El Paseo or Sun City Palm Desert.

Is this a franchise or a national directory? +

No. Local Spotlight is run by More Views Pro, a local marketing company. It's two real routes in the Coachella Valley right now, not a nationwide network.

Do you guarantee results? +

No. We can tell you exactly how many homes get the card and when it mails. We don't promise a specific number of calls or customers from it.

How do I reserve a spot? +

Scroll down and tap an open category on your route. That holds the spot and sends us your info — we'll follow up by email to arrange payment and lock it in.
