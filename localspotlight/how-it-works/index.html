WHY MAILBOXES STILL WORK

A feed scrolls past. A mailbox doesn't.

Digital ads disappear the second a thumb moves. A postcard has to be physically picked up, looked at, and either kept on the counter or thrown in the trash — there's no scrolling past it.

01

It has to be handled

Every single home on the route has to physically pick the card up out of the mailbox. That's attention a banner ad never gets.

02

No bidding war

Google and Facebook charge more every time another business outbids you for the same click. A spot on the card is a flat $300, once — it doesn't go up because a competitor wants in, because a competitor can't get in.

03

It arrives in good company

The card isn't one lonely flyer — it's 16 local businesses on one piece, the kind of lineup that reads as a trusted local directory, not a cold ad.

THE PRODUCT

What is shared postcard advertising and how does it work?

Shared postcard advertising puts several non-competing local businesses on one 11x6 postcard, with one business per category, and mails it to every home in a zone: around 5,000 households per mailing. Each business pays for its own spot, design, printing and postage are INCLUDED in the price. A 11x6 card is big enough that each business gets a real ad rather than a coupon, and the cost of the mailing splits between everybody on it.

THE CARD

11x6″, glossy, oversized, two-sided

Bigger than a standard postcard on purpose — it doesn't slip through a mail slot unnoticed. 16 spots total, 8 on the front and 8 on the back.

WHO IT REACHES

Every home on the route, not a rented list

Each route is mailed off a real USPS carrier-route report — every deliverable address in the zone, roughly 5,000 homes, not a purchased mailing list.

EXCLUSIVITY

One business per industry, per card

Reserve a spot and your industry is closed on that card for good. No competitor gets added later, no matter when they ask.

THE DESIGN

Free, and nothing prints without you

We build your spot on the card and send it over for approval first. Nothing goes to print, and nothing mails, until you sign off.

FAQ

Straight answers about how this works.

What is shared direct mail, exactly? +

One oversized postcard gets split into 16 spots, each sold to a different local business — never two businesses in the same line of work on the same card. The card is printed once and mailed to every home on the route, so you're sharing the print and postage cost instead of paying for a solo mailer.

What does a spot cost? +

$300, flat. One size, one price — there's no tiered pricing or add-on fees. That covers your spot's design, printing, and postage to every home on the route.

How does the one-business-per-industry rule work? +

Once a business reserves a spot in a given industry on a route's card, that industry is closed on that card for good — no competitor gets added later, no matter when they ask.

What areas does this cover? +

El Paseo and Sun City Palm Desert are mailing now. La Quinta is a coming-soon route you can join the waiting list for. See the live routes section for exact homeowner counts and mailing-area reports.

How do I reserve a spot? +

Open the live spot picker, pick a route, and tap an open spot on the real card image — it shows which spots are open, pending, or already sold in real time.
